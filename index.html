01 / THE RESEARCHER

About me

I am a Professor and AI Researcher at Universidad de Ingeniería y Tecnología (UTEC) in Lima, where I also coordinate the Research Master's Program in Artificial Intelligence.

My background combines a PhD and MSc in Computer Science from the University of São Paulo, two postdoctoral research appointments at UNICAMP, an MBA in Project Management, and a visiting research appointment at UC Davis.

I have led interdisciplinary R&D projects involving academic institutions and industry partners including Samsung and Shell. My interests bridge methods and applications: trustworthy machine learning, multimodal data, visual analytics, wearable sensing, digital forensics, and AI for health.

Alongside research, I am deeply committed to graduate education, academic mentorship, scientific communication, and broadening participation in AI.
